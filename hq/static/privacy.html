HQ privacy policy

HQ is a private, single-user app run by Alistair Jensen (TLLB Coaching Pty Ltd) for his own use. It is not offered to the public.

What HQ accesses

  • Google Calendar, only after the owner signs in with Google and allows it: events on the owner's calendars, to show them and to add, move or delete events at the owner's request. HQ requests no other Google data.
  • Other services the owner connects with their own keys, such as Asana, Stripe and GoHighLevel.

How it's used and stored

  • Google Calendar data is shown to the owner in HQ and used to carry out the owner's requests. It is fetched when needed and not stored, apart from the access token HQ keeps so it can stay connected.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. To answer the owner's questions in HQ's assistant, relevant data may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API for processing.
  • HQ's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Disconnect Google Calendar from HQ's Calendar tab, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Either one deletes HQ's stored token.
